What this is

SUAS is a hackathon and implementation-stage coordination system. It is our entry for the Veteran Innovation Hackathon at Hacker Dojo, 28–30 Aug 2026.

Status: SPEC-017 · NOT READY. Operations are not live. This public Pages site is not live operations.

SUAS coordinates practical support for veterans when — and only when — a later operating approval exists. Today you are reading a draft notice about a system that is not serving veterans.

What this is not

SUAS is not a medical service. It is not a mental-health service. It is not a legal service. It is not an emergency service.

  • It does not call 911.
  • It does not diagnose.
  • It does not replace VA services, county services, or 988.
  • It does not dispatch emergency responders.
  • It does not predict suicide.
  • It does not replace emergency medical care.

If you need emergency help, contact emergency services yourself. These terms do not invent official crisis wording.

No warranty

The software, these pages, and any related materials are provided as-is for demonstration and implementation review.

We make no warranty of fitness, availability, completeness, or results. The system is not ready for real operations. Do not rely on it for care, safety, legal standing, or emergency response.

Acceptable use

Do not use SUAS to harm veterans.

Do not use it to bypass consent. Consent is a purpose-scoped grant, evaluated at use time. Membership, download, and preferences are not consent. There is no blanket "I agree" that opens every scope.

Do not treat these pages as permission to operate, enroll real veterans, or contact real providers.
