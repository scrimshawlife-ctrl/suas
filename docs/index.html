28 AUG 2026 PT

  1. This walk. A short click-through of the idea lives on this page. It is not a live app and it does not call a public host.
  2. Check-In. A veteran can start, answer, and complete a Check-In. Completing it asks for a score.
  3. Support case. A settled red score opens or updates a support case. Yellow and orange do not. SUAS does not call 911.
  4. Consent. Grant, revoke, and list exist in the product. This walk does not show them.
  5. Queue. A red case can sit unassigned. One responder can claim it.
  6. Peer backup. Deploy and cancel exist in the product. They are not in this walk.
  7. On duty. The board says unavailable. There is no stored roster.
  8. Sends. Rides, rooms, food, SMS, and email are not sent for real.
  9. Stage. A synthetic staging host exists. Production stays closed. Not ready for a pilot.

STILL IN FORMATION

  1. Real rides and rooms stay off.
  2. Food and peer APIs wait on later decisions.
  3. Admin cannot invent a new vendor from the screen.

Not: EHR, diagnosis, suicide prediction, automated emergency dispatch, HIPAA claim.
