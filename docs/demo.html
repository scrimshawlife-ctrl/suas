Check-In

Pick how urgent this is.

A Check-In is a short form a veteran fills out. You pick how urgent it is: yellow, orange, or red. This page does not score a real person.

Yellow and orange do not open a support case. Red can. A support case is a record that someone needs help now. The result stays on this screen.
